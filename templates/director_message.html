Message from the Director

Dr. Makarand Madhao Ghangrekar

Director, National Institute of Technology Puducherry

Dear Distinguished Alumni,

I trust this message finds you in good health and high spirits. As the Director of National Institute of Technology Puducherry, it is both an honor and a privilege to connect with you—our esteemed alumni—whose achievements continue to illuminate the legacy of our institution.

The passage of time only deepens our admiration for the remarkable strides you have made in your respective fields. Your successes not only reflect the rigorous education and formative experiences gained here but also serve as a powerful testament to the enduring values of excellence, integrity, and innovation that define our alma mater.

Key Developments at NIT Puducherry:

  • Pioneering Research and Innovation: Groundbreaking research initiatives are underway, focusing on global challenges with innovative solutions.
  • Enhanced Infrastructure: Substantial investments have been made in cutting-edge laboratories, modernized learning spaces, and advanced digital resources.
  • Strengthening Alumni Engagement: New programs like alumni networking events, guest lectures, and mentorship opportunities are being launched.
  • Global Collaborations: Strategic partnerships with leading global entities are expanding research, learning, and cultural exchange opportunities.
  • NIRF Rank: With significant contributions from alumni, NITPY has secured the 97th rank among engineering institutes in India, entering the top 100.

As we move forward, I earnestly invite you to renew and strengthen your ties with NIT Puducherry. Your insights, experiences, and contributions are critical to our shared mission of fostering a thriving intellectual community.

I encourage you to engage with us through our enhanced alumni portal, where you can access updates, share your milestones, and participate in upcoming events. Together, we can ensure that NIT Puducherry remains a beacon of excellence for generations to come.

Thank you for your unwavering support and commitment. I look forward to the many ways we can collaborate and celebrate our shared achievements in the years ahead.

With warmest regards,

Dr. Makarand Madhao Ghangrekar
Director, National Institute of Technology Puducherry
